Git is the industry-standard version control system
 designed to ensure you never lose your work, 
primarily by replacing your actual code with a labyrinth
 of cryptic terminal commands and detached HEAD states. 
It masterfully transforms the simple act of saving a file into a high-stakes 
psychological thriller where one wrong git push --force can obliterate a 
coworker's entire career. Nothing cements team bonding quite like spending 
four hours resolving merge conflicts on a Friday afternoon, only to realize you have
 absolutely no idea what a rebase does, why your branch is yelling at you, or how to exit Vim.
